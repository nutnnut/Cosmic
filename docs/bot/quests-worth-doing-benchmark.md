# Quests Worth Doing - community benchmark (MapleRoyals, v83-era)

Source: https://royals.ms/forum/threads/quests-worth-doing-2-0.185825/ (fetched 2026-06-13).
Purpose: ground truth for the bot quest advisor - quests a v83 community considers worth
the time (easy, high exp, fast, low travel, doable unfunded). The advisor should DISCOVER
most of these as high-value via its own exp/time + opportunity-cost scoring, NOT hardcode
them; use this list to tune/validate worthwhile thresholds (treat RANKINGS as signal -
Royals runs an exp multiplier, and authoritative rewards come from Quest.wz Act.img at
runtime anyway; the exp column here is best-effort scraped and partially empty).
Scripted/jump-quest/PQ/exchange entries are out of scope for bot slices 1-2.
