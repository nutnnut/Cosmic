Scroll valuation v2 — proposed vs SoloMapling

STATUS: shipped. v2 implemented in BotScrollManager.scrollCombatCeilingMeso / marketStatValue and the scroll branch of BotInventoryManager.useShelfKeepValue (max(sellback, min(obtainCost, combatCeiling))). This page is the calibration record.

Updated per: our shops don't sell these (same Cosmic base — the shopitems rows are inaccessible/GM, so obtain cost = drop-farm, not a cheap shop price) and PER_EV tripled 250k → 750k to float toward SoloMapling.

Formula (unchanged shape, one knob retuned)

combatCeiling = 750,000 × (successRate) × Σ(statBonus × bestBuyerWeight)
our_propose   = max(npcSellBack, min(farmCost, combatCeiling))

bestBuyerWeight (SSOT offense weights, job-agnostic): PAD=5, STR/DEX/INT/LUK=1, MAD=1, jump≈0.1. ev = (sr/100) × statBonus × weight. With shops gone, farmCost is the only supply cap and it's high for these rare scrolls, so for most rows combatCeiling is what binds → our_propose ≈ ceiling (noted where farm would still cap).

Read on the tripled fit

  1. Primary-stat scrolls now nail it — Overall DEX 60% 1.0×, DEX 10% 1.1×, Cape STR 10% 1.1×, the Cape 10%s all 0.6–0.8×. Tripling lined the bulk of the table up with SoloMapling.
  2. Physical attack scrolls overshoot 3–4×. Pure consequence of ATT_WEIGHT=5: SoloMapling's real attack premium over a stat point is only ~2–3×, ours is 5×. You can match attack or stat at a single PER_EV, not both — at 250k attack matched and stat undershot; at 750k stat matches and attack overshoots.
  3. INT/MAD undershoot 0.3–0.4×. Two stacked causes: MATK_WEIGHT=1 (mage attack treated as a plain stat) plus SoloMapling's class-balance INT premium. Both are weight/curation, not fixable by PER_EV.
  4. Jump stays ~0.05× — near-zero combat weight. SoloMapling's 0.5–1M for a jump scroll is mobility curation we don't model; arguably they're the outlier here.
The real lever is the attack weight, not PER_EV. If you want attack scrolls to land near SoloMapling too, the scroll ceiling needs a compressed attack weight (~2–3) rather than the full DPS-model 5. That's a market-pricing choice distinct from the SSOT combat weight — I can add a scroll-local ATT_MARKET_WEIGHT without touching offenseValueFromStats.
