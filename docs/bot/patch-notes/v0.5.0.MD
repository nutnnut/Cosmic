# Bot Patch Notes v0.5.0 (Economy Update)

Bots now participate in a living player-driven economy, while grinding and simulation have been tuned for larger, more natural bot populations.

## 1. New Features

- **Living bot economy** - bots now keep track of market prices, item demand, and recent trades when making gear and inventory decisions.
- **Free Market stalls** - autonomous bots can travel to the Free Market, browse merchants, set up stalls, list spare equipment, collect proceeds, and return to their previous activity.
- **Bot shout trading** - bots can advertise equipment and respond to matching trade shouts, allowing direct equipment trades with other bots and players.
- **More natural market activity** - idle bots may visit the Free Market, browse as visitors, and contribute to a busier market.
- **Larger bot populations** - bots outside active player observation can use a lighter simulation path while preserving progression, allowing more bots to run at once.

## 2. Changes / Improvements

- **Smarter grinding** - bots choose tighter grind spots and adapt between camping, patrolling, stacking, and roaming instead of wandering across the whole map.
- **Class-aware positioning** - bots choose more suitable positions and engagement patterns for their class, movement skills, and attack style.
- **Improved gear progression** - shared gear scoring better recognizes cross-slot upgrades, weapon speed, defensive value, and useful drops when choosing what to farm or equip.
- **Improved auto-scrolling** - scroll decisions better account for item quality, slot longevity, and whether a better base item is worth waiting for.
- **Better bot efficiency** - reduced simulation work for unobserved bots and improved performance across common grind and save paths.

## 3. Bug Fixes

- Fixed various cases of bots getting stuck during existing autopilot travel, map transitions, and uneven terrain.
- Fixed some grind decisions using stale or mismatched equipment values.
- Fixed edge cases in existing auto-scroll valuation and item handling.
- General stability improvements for long-running bot sessions.
