# Bot Patch Notes v0.4.0 (Preliminary / The wishful version)

The big one: bots can now go play on their own, grind their own equips, independently.

## 1. New Features

- **Bot can travel the world!** - walking through portals, taking ferries / taxis / return scrolls across maps and continents instead of teleporting/rubberbanding to the owner.
- **Bot autonomy / autopilot** - tell a bot (or a whole bot party) to go off and play independently. It travels across maps on its own, picks a good place to grind, farms, resupplies when low, progress gears, and keeps going - no babysitting required.
- **Take over your own characters** - `@botme` turns the character you're on into a bot; `@botparty` does it for a full party of your alts.
- **Bot gear progression** - now semi-automatic/automatic from multiple source (find map the drop upgrades, maker skills, gachapon)
- **Auto-grind gears** - autopilot mode consider equip drops/gear progression into the planner
- **Auto-sell junk** - now also sell use and etc inventories (valuables/scrolls/crafting materials protected)
- **Auto-scrolling** - Auto decide and semi-optimally scroll items
- **Auto-questing** - Auto decide and do quests (only simple kill/fetch quests supported)
- **Auto-crafting/disassembling** - bots can plan and craft gear upgrades for themselves (Maker skill)
- **Auto-job advance** - (Simplified) walk to job instructor and job advance(without doing quests)
- **Auto-Gachapon** - bots spend looted NX at the best gachapon, chasing useful pulls.
- **Dynamic Partying** - bots occasionally invite other bots and players to their party, player can request to join party.
- **Bot Ice Movements** - bot now slip believably on snowy maps, including countersteering to navigate
- **Farm item command** - `farm <item>` sends bots toward whatever map best drops that item
- **World Map Webserver + RTS Mode** - Find and command bots from web browser

## 1b. Fully autonomous / "ownerless" bots

A bot can now be spawned with no human owner and grow up entirely on its own.

- **Ownerless spawn** - `@spawnbot <name> autopilot` spawns a self-owned bot that immediately starts playing on its own; `@spawnbot generate ...` invents a procedural MMO name (and auto-creates the account/character) so you can stamp out a crowd of them. Fresh ones start at level 1 in Mushroom Town, like a real new player.

## 1c. Living-server population / auto generate "ownerless" bots (`!botpop`)

A background scheduler can keep a crowd of server-generated bots logging in and out on their own so the world feels alive. **Off by default**; only bots made with `@spawnbot generate` are ever managed (named bots / `@botme` / `@registerbot` are never auto-scheduled).

- **Persistent crews** - some bots spawn as a friend group that arrives together, parties up, and shares gear/ammo/supplies like an owned party. Bots also dynamically party up with each other (and proactively invite a co-located real player), trait-gated so they don't yank into real-player parties uninvited.

## 2. Changes / Improvements

- Up to 20x map graph warmup/generation speed.
- Better inventory housekeeping: reports on what got sold, never-sell protection for good gear, and cleanup of stale quest items. Adjustments on what gets kept/sold
- **Smarter grinding** - accuracy-aware map/target choice (avoids mobs it can't hit), commits to a target instead of flip-flopping, dodges mobs while walking, and steps a little closer to land a stronger skill instead of spamming a weaker long-range one.
- **Self-preservation** - backs off mobs whose touch damage would kill it, with risk-aware travel (low-level penalty, Sleepywood blocked) and a death-loop breaker that escapes to town and shuns the lethal route.
- **Smarter supplies & economy** - pressure-driven "sell from a value shelf" model for the USE bag, class-weighted pot budget at shops, buys worthwhile gear upgrades from NPC shops, and shares **summoning/magic rocks** like potions when low (and uses rock-consuming buffs sparingly on trash mobs).
- **Smarter scrolling** - weighs the *opportunity cost* of each scroll (won't burn a good scroll for a tiny bump) and **holds scrolls when a clearly-better base is farmable**; proactively offers scrolls useless to itself but useful to a partymate (bow scroll -> archer, INT scroll -> mage).
- **Party catch-up** - in a `@botparty`, bots that out-level the group idle on a safe spot (do no damage) so the lower bots get full EXP share and catch up.
- Other performance optimizations
