# Bot Patch Notes v0.5.1 (Ambition Update)

Bots now chase long-horizon goals of their own: they work the Temple of Time questline, hunt down the fourth-job skill books their build actually needs, and behave more like people while doing it. This patch also lands a large round of performance and travel-reliability work for big populations.

## 1. New Features

- **Temple of Time questline** - high-level autonomous bots decide, at their own personal ambition level, to start chipping away at the Temple of Time mainline. They pick up quests, grind the corridor lanes for their kill quotas, buy and drink the Sorcerer's Potion, farm the miniboss helmets, and open the corridor toward Pink Bean one gate at a time. Bots trickle in at different levels and spread out when a lane is already crowded instead of swarming it.
- **Dragon flight to Leafre** - bots can now take the Halfling's dragon ride between the Leafre dock and the Temple of Time, morphing into a dragon and flying the corridor like a player does.
- **Skill and mastery books** - bots understand fourth-job books. They know which books their chosen build will eventually need, keep them instead of selling them, read them when they qualify, and hand spare books to a party member or sibling bot who can use one. Needed books are also weighed when choosing where to farm, what to buy at a Free Market stall, and what to shout for.
- **Chairs** - idle bots resting in town or standing around the Free Market may plop into a chair they own from gachapon. Loud, attention-seeking bots go for the biggest seat they own; quiet ones rarely sit at all. They always stand up before doing anything active, and a hit knocks them out of the chair.
- **Obnoxious shouting** - some bots pad their trade shouts to inflate their chat bubble, the way certain Free Market regulars do. How much depends on the bot's personality.
- **Return scroll restocking** - bots keep a small stock of Return to Nearest Town scrolls and restock when they run low, instead of only buying them opportunistically.

## 2. Changes / Improvements

- **Need-first shop finding** - when a bot heads to town it now picks the shop that covers the most of what it actually needs right now, searching both around itself and around its home town hub, so department-store side rooms get used instead of being walked past.
- **Cleaner shout trading** - a bot advertising a buy order no longer answers sellers and buyers at the same time, and a bot advertising a specific piece of gear will only sell that exact piece rather than substituting something else when a buyer walks up.
- **Honest market prices** - the shared price consensus is now seeded only by trades where every side that could move the price was a bot, so a player naming any price on their own stall (or haggling a bot up and down) can no longer skew what the whole bot population believes an item is worth. Those trades still show up on the market tape.
- **Correct mage casting speed** - magic attacks no longer read the wand or staff's speed tier (the client always casts at Normal speed, adjusted only by Spell Booster). Mage weapon scoring is speed-blind to match, so bots stop over-valuing "fast" wands.
- **Skill books on the gear scale** - a needed book is priced by the same value model as an equipment upgrade, so a bot can decide a Genesis book is worth more farming time than a hat.
- **Steadier gear suggestions** - the gear/scroll offer pass no longer re-runs a full equipment scan every status check, which was a large chunk of idle CPU at population scale.
- **Much lower population CPU** - a wide sweep of hot spots: cached bag and potion scans, cached skill names and spawn data, a faster equipment band curve, cheaper grind-map admission, a cheaper nearby-quest scan, lock-free performance counters, and a much cheaper clock on the tick path.
- **Better performance reporting** - `/api/perf` samples now survive long windows intact and split measured bot CPU from everything else, making it far easier to tell bot work from server work.

## 3. Bug Fixes

- Fixed bots looping forever on the World Tour ride when their saved return location pointed back into Zipangu.
- Fixed bots committing to a stale travel hop chosen before navigation data had warmed up, and re-checking that hop forever afterwards.
- Fixed bots failing to reach the Leafre and Temple of Time area.
- Fixed collision and pit portals: bots now use the same trigger box the client does, correctly fire touch-script portals, and reset their physics after being warped by one.
- Fixed walk-off drops being planned from the wrong platform when another platform sat just overhead, which left bots parked or oscillating at ledges (navigation graph v71).
- Fixed repeated false anti-cheat warnings and autoban alerts caused by stray equipment stranded in a bot's worn-equipment slots; such items are now moved back into the bag.
- Fixed companion bots not recovering their autopilot after their owner logged out.
- Fixed a live operator command being overwritten by a routine party plan rebuild, or cut short by an automatic logout.
- Fixed the Free Market trip planner ignoring a bot's own outstanding wants when deciding whether the trip was worth making.
- Fixed stacked items being listed as a single all-or-nothing lot when selling books.
- Fixed a bot re-buying an item it had already replaced, and retiring its buy order on an unrelated purchase.
